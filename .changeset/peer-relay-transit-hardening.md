"@oa-sdk/peer-server": minor
"@oa-sdk/peer-transport": minor

Harden the WebSocket relay and Cloud Transit, and make the transit client and WebSocket adapter follow the relay's lifecycle. Review the items marked **behavior change** when upgrading.

- `@oa-sdk/peer-server`: the relay no longer parses the client `Host` header of an upgrade; a malformed one used to throw in the upgrade listener and end the process. Relay packets are forwarded only after the provider approved the room (the sender is closed with `RELAY_NOT_APPROVED` otherwise), and a peer that sends a signal only the relay issues (`ready`, `request`, `approved`, `released`, `closed`) is refused with `FORBIDDEN_SIGNAL`. Both used to reach the other side unchanged. The provider decision's `allowedTools` is enforced: a client `tools/call` outside it, or a JSON-RPC batch that contains one, is answered with a `TOOL_NOT_ALLOWED` (-32602) error and never forwarded.
- `@oa-sdk/peer-server` **behavior change:** `handleCloudTransitHttpRequest` and `handleCloudTransitWorker` enforce the `publishKey` and `allowedOrigins` they are given. A request from an `Origin` that is not allowed gets 403 without CORS headers, and, when `publishKey` is set, a request other than a preflight without the key (`X-Peer-Publish-Key` or `Authorization: Bearer`) gets 401. Without `allowedOrigins` only exact loopback origins are allowed; requests without an `Origin` header are not subject to the origin check. Both handlers used to ignore the options and echo any `Origin`. The CORS allow list now includes `X-Target-Peer`. `CloudTransitProxy.authorize()` is added, `defaultAlias` is public, and the `./cloud` entry point re-exports `PeerRoomManager`.
- `@oa-sdk/peer-server` **behavior change:** `POST /p/:roomId/join` authenticates with the room's invite token (`inviteToken` in the body, or `Authorization: Bearer`) instead of the server-wide `publishKey`, which made every invite join on a key-protected server fail with 401. An unknown room gets 404 instead of 400.
- `@oa-sdk/peer-server` **behavior change:** an approved relay session ends after `relayIdleTimeoutMs` without relay frames (default 5 minutes, 0 disables) or after `relayMaxSessionMs` (default 24 hours) instead of 60 seconds after approval; the 60-second window now bounds only offer, answer and release. `PeerRelayManager` honors `roomTtlMs` and throws `RangeError` for an invalid time option, also when `createPeerServer` creates it. Upgrades to `/ws`, `/relay` or `/p/...` without a valid room or role get 400, and other upgrade paths get 404 when no other upgrade listener exists; such upgrades used to hang. Socket listeners are attached before the ticket check, so early frames and closes are not lost, an expired room closes both sockets, and `POST /call` answers 413 for a call too large for the relay instead of ending the provider's relay session.
- `@oa-sdk/peer-server`: the new `RelayTransitBridge`, which `createPeerServer` attaches when both `enableRelay` and `enableCloudTransit` are set, lets Cloud Transit (`/transit/:peer/mcp`, `/call`) reach a provider that joins the relay with `alias=<peer-alias>`. New exports: `RelayTransitBinder`, `MAX_SIGNAL_FRAMES`, `SIGNALING_WINDOW_MS`, `DEFAULT_RELAY_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS`, `DEFAULT_RELAY_MAX_SESSION_MS`, `SignalingRoomOptions`, and the `PeerRelayManager` methods `attachVirtualClient`, `relayToProvider`, `endRoom` and the now public `pruneExpiredRooms`.
- `@oa-sdk/peer-transport`: `parseSignalMessage` applies the 64 KiB signal limit by the parsed `type`, so a frame that merely contains `"type":"relay"` no longer bypasses it. It rebuilds envelopes without unknown members and throws `TypeError` for malformed ones. The `approved` signal gains `allowedTools?: string[]`.
- `@oa-sdk/peer-transport` **behavior change:** `CloudTransitClient.listTools(peerAlias?, { signal, timeoutMs }?)` POSTs a JSON-RPC `tools/list` to `/transit/:peer/mcp` instead of `GET /tools`, returns the new `CloudTransitTool[]`, and throws `Failed to list tools: ...` on an HTTP or JSON-RPC error, a timeout or a cancellation. `callTool` sends nothing for an already-aborted signal and reports a user abort as `TOOL_CALL_CANCELLED` instead of a timeout.
- `@oa-sdk/peer-transport` **behavior change:** the `connect()` of `createWebSocketTransportAdapter` resolves on the relay's `ready` signal instead of on socket open, and rejects when the ticket is refused or the socket closes first. The channel is connected only after `approved`, and relay packets received before approval are dropped. The adapter no longer reconnects after `disconnect()`, `dispose()`, a terminal signal or a 1000/1008 close, `connect()` after `dispose()` rejects, and a stale `onclose` no longer overwrites a newer connection.
