"@oa-sdk/webmcp-peer": minor
---

An approval request now waits for a person, not for an RPC timeout; a host that keeps its socket for the room's whole life can still reclaim the room after a reload; the provider learns of every decision, expiry and revocation the relay makes outside its socket; and a revocation ends the session's requests in flight on both transports (relay protocol spec O-06, O-07, O-10, O-17, O-18, O-19, O-50, O-51). Several of these change what a peer sees, so review the items marked **Behavior change**.

- **Behavior change:** the reconnect lease does not expire while a provider socket is attached. Once the room has no provider socket (it was just created or reclaimed, or its socket closed or was detached), the lease lasts 15 minutes more, or until the room expires. Before, the lease expired 15 minutes after the room was created or reclaimed, so a host that stayed connected longer got 410 `ROOM_LEASE_EXPIRED` on its reload and lost the agent's ticket to a new room. A reclaim while the provider socket is attached is now 409 `ROOM_PROVIDER_ACTIVE` at any time (it was 410 `ROOM_LEASE_EXPIRED` after the first 15 minutes).
- **Behavior change:** the room descriptor of `POST /p`, reclaim and rotate reports `reconnectTokenExpiresAt` as the room's `expiresAt` (the latest the lease can be used) instead of creation or reclaim time plus 15 minutes, and adds `reconnectGraceMs: 900000`, the lease's grace without a provider socket. The provider keeps a saved lease until `reconnectTokenExpiresAt`, as before.
- **Behavior change:** an approval request lives for the new `WebMcpPeerServerOptions.approvalTimeoutMs` (default 300000 ms, at least 1000; `Infinity` waits as long as the room lives; never past the room's expiry), not for `mcpRequestTimeoutMs` (30 s by default), which now only bounds relayed MCP requests. `WebMcpPeerServer.approvalTimeoutMs` reports the value in use.
- **Behavior change:** when an approval request expires, the room's provider socket gets the same `approval.expired` frame as the agent's client sockets, so the host takes the request down (the provider ends its session with `APPROVAL_EXPIRED`). Before, the provider was told nothing and offered a decision the relay then refused.
- **Behavior change:** an approval timer that fires before its request is due (Node timers can fire a little early, and a delay over 2147483647 ms fires at once) is set again for the time left. Before, such a request never expired and its ticket stayed pending until something else ended it. A timer that finds the room expired carries out the room's expiry.
- **Behavior change:** a decision made over HTTP (`POST /p/:id/approval/:requestId`) is echoed to the room's provider socket with the same `approval.approved`/`approval.rejected` frame a decision on that socket gets. Each decision is echoed once, whichever way it was made.
- **Behavior change:** every revocation (the agent's own, the provider's single-session revoke, the revoke of every session) sends the room's provider socket the `session.revoked` frame the agent's client sockets get, with the relay's fixed reason (`CLIENT_REVOKED`, `SESSION_REVOKED`, `ROOM_REVOKED`). The provider ends its session as revoked, as it does for its own `revokeClient()`.
- **Behavior change:** the provider takes the relay's `approval.approved` as the ticket's approval even when it did not decide it (an approval over HTTP), after the checks it applies to `approval.restored` (a session, this room's epoch and room, a ticket expiry in the future, read from `ticketExpiresAt`). Before, such an approval left the provider pending, and it refused the approved agent with `CLIENT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`.
- **Behavior change:** the provider undoes a decision the relay refuses: an `INVALID_APPROVAL` error frame for the request of the host's latest decision, arriving before the relay confirmed that decision, puts the provider back to `pending` for that request with the session error `INVALID_APPROVAL`. Before, the provider stayed `approved` (or `revoked`) while the relay's ticket was not.
- **Behavior change:** a single-session revoke (by the provider or by the agent itself), like every other ending of a ticket, ends the ticket's HTTP `POST /p/:id/mcp` requests in flight with the error its WebSocket requests get (−32001 `SESSION_INVALID` for a revocation). Before, an HTTP request in flight kept waiting and got the provider's answer after the revoke. A request the provider already took may still run there; the relay drops its late answer. `PendingMcpRequest` has a new `ticket` member, the ticket that authorized the request.
- **Behavior change:** `roomId` and `roomEpoch` are optional in the body of every route that changes a room (reclaim, approval, both revoke routes, rotate) and, when present, must be the room's own. Approval no longer requires them (it answered 409 `INVALID_APPROVAL` without them), and neither do the agent's revoke and the provider's path-form session revoke (409 `SESSION_BINDING_INVALID`). The provider's revoke of every session (`POST /p/:id/session/revoke`) now refuses a body naming another room or epoch with 409 `SESSION_BINDING_INVALID` instead of revoking every session. Each route keeps its error name.
- **Behavior change:** the room binding is checked after the route's credential: a path-form session revoke with a wrong token and a wrong binding is 401 `CLIENT_TOKEN_INVALID` (it was 409 `SESSION_BINDING_INVALID`), and a reclaim with a wrong lease and a wrong binding is 403 `ROOM_LEASE_INVALID` (it was 409 `ROOM_LEASE_BINDING_INVALID`).
- **Behavior change:** the provider's `revokeClient()` (`disconnect(true)`) leaves `roomEpoch` out of its session revoke when it does not know the room's epoch, instead of sending 0, which the relay refuses.
- **Behavior change:** the HTTP approval response adds `approvalExpiresAt` and `ticketExpiresAt` (epoch ms) next to its ISO `expiresAt` (the approval request's expiry), and its `decidedAt` is the decision time the ticket recorded. The client's `approval.approved` and the provider's decision echo add the same two members at the top level and in `payload`, because their `expiresAt` means the ticket's expiry in one and the request's in the other. Existing time fields keep their formats; new ones are epoch ms.
- **Behavior change:** `approval.pending` and `approval.expired` carry `protocol`, `messageId` and `sentAt` like the relay's other control frames.
- **Behavior change:** every relay `messageId` is an opaque string unique to its message (`<type>-<32 hex>`), made by the new protected `WebMcpPeerServer.messageId(type)`. Before, ids were built from request and session ids (`approval-approval-…`, `approval-restored-<sessionId>` repeated on every reattach, `<type>-<sessionId>-<ms>`). Compare message ids; do not parse them.
- The spec now states as normative what the relay already did: a revoke of every session answers `{roomId, revoked: true, revokedSessions: true}`, a single revoke is idempotent (`alreadyRevoked`) and echoes the caller's `reason` to the caller alone, the `session.revoked` frames carry the relay's fixed reason, a repeated approval decision is 409 `INVALID_APPROVAL`, and without a publish key the owner hints of `POST /p` are never the room's owner while two contradicting hints are refused with 403 `PUBLISH_OWNER_BINDING_INVALID`.
- The live demo's host clears its pending approval on the relay's decision echo, `approval.expired` and `session.revoked`.
- Protected relay API: `approveClientTicket` sends the decision echo itself (the provider socket handler no longer does); new protected `startReconnectGrace`, `messageId`, `bodyNamesRoom`, `scheduleApprovalExpiry`, `expireApprovalRequest`, `decisionTimes` and `sendDecisionEcho`.
